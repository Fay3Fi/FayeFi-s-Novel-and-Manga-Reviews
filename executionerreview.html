The Executioner and Her Way of Life

The Executioner and Her Way of Life is an Action, Adventure, Romance, Isekai light novel series written by Mato Sato and illustrated by Nilitsu. It follows Menou, an Executioner for the otherworld’s church whose job it is to execute any otherworlders that come in from earth due to their powers being far too unstable and potentially world ending if they go out of control. After a summoning ritual summons two otherworlders, she kills one and goes to kill the other, Akari Tokitou, but she fails due to Akari’s power, that being the pure concept of time, making her unkillable. The pair go on a journey, with Menou lying to Akari saying she’s trying to help her go home, when in reality she’s trying to find a way to kill her, and Akari fully believing everything she says and being strangely attached to Menou from the get-go.

I love the world building in this series, the whole idea of people from earth getting transported to another world and getting extremely strong powers is a very widely used trope and is getting a little tiring, so I love how this series twists that by making the otherworlders a relatively common thing instead of just a select few, and how the powers (called pure concepts) have a major downside, being that the more they use them, the more their memories and personality fade away, until they become an empty shell only acting in accordance to the pure concept they have, called a Human Error. The series feature 4 landmarks called the Major Human Errors, these are otherworlders who went out of control and became so powerful and dangerous that they had to be sealed away, with the seals acting as landmarks, but also to the fall of the od civilisation at the hands of pure concepts. These human errors and the whole idea of pure concepts not only give a cool twist on the Isekai genre, but also provide a reason why the executioners exist, even if only in secret. There’s plenty of other cool worldbuilding in the series but this is my favourite part.

There’s one specific villain that I’m a fan of, that being Pandæmonium, one of the 4 major human errors. She is quite literally evil incarnate, having the pure concept of evil and having become a human error. She is really creepy, falling into the classic creepy little girl trope that you see a lot in horror, although she leans a lot toward body horror instead, she also simultaneously falls into my two favourite types of villains, pure evil ones and ones with tragic backstories. The story of the person who was Pandæmonium is pretty sad, but now that the pure concept has fully taken over, she’s just straight up evil. The way her powers work relies heavily on sacrificing living beings and either creating or turning them into monsters, but that is not where the body horror comes from, it comes from her making herself pseudo-immortal by sacrificing her own corpse to bring herself back to life, often clawing out of her old body like a messed-up chest burster from Alien, which is the coolest and creepiest form of immortality I’ve seen. Whenever she shows up, chaos always follows, making her very entertaining but also creepy as hell. She also acts as a motivator for Menou, showing what kind of thing Akari could turn into if she uses her powers, and fuels her motivation to kill her, not out of duty but out of mercy.

This series is great, with a total of 11 volumes to sink your teeth in, I highly recommend it. There is also a manga, which I have yet to read but it doesn’t look like it adapts everything which kinda sucks, and there’s an anime which, while it only covers the first 2 volumes, is still pretty good aside from some bad animation at points.

Rating: 8/10
