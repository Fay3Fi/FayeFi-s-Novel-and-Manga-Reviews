Otherside Picnic

Otherside Picnic is a Horror, Action, Romance light novel series written by Iori Miyazawa with illustrations by Shirakaba. The series follows Sorawo Kamikoshi and Toriko Nishina, two university students, as they explore a mysterious hidden world called the Otherside that exists separate from the real world. They explore this world for profit and to find Toriko’s missing friend who suddenly vanished 3 months prior. In the Otherside, they encounter mysterious entities that take the form of creatures from various ghost stories and folklore legends in an attempt to drive people mad with fear. Throughout the series, they get closer to one another and their relationship develops in a slow-burn romance.

Out of all the media I have seen (books, movies, tv, games, etc.), This series stands as my favourite. I love almost everything about it and have very few complaints (most of which might not apply in future due to it being an ongoing series). The dynamic between the main two is great and very unique, seeing Sorawo grapple with Toriko’s feelings after volume 3 is great, and despite it being 5 volumes long, it doesn’t feel drawn out at all. Sorawo is a very well written character (as are all of the characters), and her unique stance on romance is a breath of fresh air (and even little relatable for me). Their relationship is a very unique one, even from the start, so I'm glad that they don’t fall into a typical romantic relationship once they do finally get together, instead settling on something special reserved only for them.

While I don’t necessarily find a lot of the things in the series scary (excluding Satsuki Uruma from that, she’s creepy as hell), I do think the scares and the things they go up against are very unique, I’ve never seen a series take inspiration from ghost stories in this way, and its important to note that they aren’t just completely ripped from the original stories, the Otherside entities are fundamentally different and are just taking their forms after all, that’s not even mentioning how they take some creative liberties with the stories as well (a good example being Kisaragi Station). The action in the series is also really unique, the use of Sorawo’s eye and Toriko’s hand (both of which got powers thanks to a close call in the Otherside) to reveal the true forms of the entities and make them killable is really cool. I also like how, once the entities true forms have been revealed, they just use normal guns against them and it works perfectly (most of the time), it’s a really funny idea that shooting an unknowable entity with a normal gun is able to kill it, and they do give a reason why this works later in the series and it is a very cool and fascinating reason.

To conclude, this series is awesome and love it. I do wish we got some more explanation of Sorawo’s past, and maybe a bonus chapter from Toriko’s perspective, but aside from those small complaints that could be addressed in future volumes, I think this series is nearly perfect (IN MY OPINION). I haven’t even scratched the surface of all my thoughts on this series, but I need to keep this from getting too long. I also want to mention that there is a really good manga adaptation (its not as good as the novels but its still really good), and there is a mediocre anime adaptation (its alright if you haven’t checked out the series before, but its pretty bad if you have, overall, just a bad adaptation). Seriously go read this series if you haven’t, it’s amazing.

Rating: 10/10
