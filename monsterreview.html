This Monster Wants to Eat Me

This Monster Wants to Eat Me is a Horror, Drama manga series written and illustrated by Sai Naekawa. The series follows Hinako Yaotose, a depressed girl who is suicidal yet can’t bring herself to go through with it. After almost dying and being saved from a monster, her saviour introducers herself as Shiori Oumi, a mermaid, she tells Hinako that she has come to eat her, but only once Hinako tastes her absolute best (Aka, when she is happy again). This starts a strange and deadly depression healing journey, all the while secrets of Shiori true nature are slowly revealed.

Hinako’s depression is very well depicted in this series, there are multiple times where she begins disassociating and the world starts to look like its underwater, a clear connection to the incident that caused her depression. She also never quite gets over it, she even gets worse at some points (like when Shiori’s true goal was revealed), I'm glad that it never quite goes away as it would be cheap and very unrealistic if it did everything was solved. Depression (especially this bad) is not easy to get over, maybe even impossible to, but it can get better, even if it’s a slow process, this is clearly shown in this series. The suicidal ideation is also very well done, she can’t bring herself to do it and wishes for something like the ocean that killed her family to kill her, something beautiful, deadly, uncontrollable and unknowable, all of these line up perfectly with Shiori. This creates a strong co-dependency between the two, yet it also creates a wall between them, as they are mostly just using each other for their own gain.

The art in this series is also gorgeous. The monster designs are amazing, especially Shiori’s true form, which is actually quite scary. Whenever the monsters are on screen, the art is always visceral and just overall incredible, the reveal of Shiori’s true form has some of the best art I’ve seen in a manga. The horror is also very well done, being one of the only manga’s to actually scare me a bit. Ironically, the scariest thing in this series is actually something that looks like a human, Azami, her empty eyes and near constant smile always creep me out. The art is also good with facial expressions, some panels have so much emotions packed into the characters faces. In a recent chapter, Hinako has a sad expression in one of the panels, and you're able to see that this is different from the other sad expressions in the series, you could tell that it wasn’t caused by her depression even if you removed the context of the scene.

I highly recommend this series, fair warning however, it does get pretty heavy at a lot of points, however this should be obvious due to some of the subject matter. Yet again I haven’t been able to say all of my thoughts, but I hope this is enough to show how much I like this series. There is also an anime adaptation which is actually really good, there is some pacing and animation issues but other than that it is really well done, I highly recommend the anime too. if I had to say anything more, I hope this series has a happy ending.

Rating: 8/10
