FayeFi's Novel and Manga Reviews

Hi, Im FayeFi, and this is a website made for a college assessment where I will review some of my favourite Manga/Novels and give them a rating out of 10.

I tend to read alot of romance series (mostly Yuri, Im a bit of a Himejoshi) so the series listed will all be in that genre, though they will fall into other genres and some may put less focus on the romance over other things.

Expect some spoilers in the reviews, its probably stupid to recommend these series whilst also spoiling them at the same time, but I digress.

Im probably not the best at analysing, or writing out my thoughts, but I just wanted an excuse to talk even just a little about the series that I love (especially one of them) and this college assessment was the perfect opportunity.

I will be going over these series:

  • Otherside Picnic (Novel)
  • The Moon on a Rainy Night (Manga)
  • This Monster Wants to Eat Me (Manga)
  • I See You, Aizawa-san! (Manga)
  • The Executioner and Her Way of Life (Novel)
